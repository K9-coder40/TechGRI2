About TechGri

Connecting Agricultural Education & Extension with modern web innovation.

Our Foundation

Bridging the Extension Gap

TechGri was conceived to address a key structural challenge in agriculture: delivering timely, practical agronomic knowledge directly to smallholders while opening transparent market avenues.

By merging Agricultural Education and Extension principles with modern responsive web engineering, TechGri turns extension guides, local produce listings, and AI tools into a single, user-friendly digital hub.

Driven By Purpose

Our core pillars driving agricultural transformation

Our Mission

To empower farmers with localized digital advisories, interactive learning tools, and direct market access that maximizes farmgate yield and income.

Our Vision

A connected rural agricultural community where information flows seamlessly between farmers, extension officers, and traders.

Our Core Values

Community empowerment, practical science-backed agronomy, transparent pricing, and accessible digital technology for all.

Who TechGri Serves

Building an inclusive ecosystem for all stakeholders in the value chain

Local Farmers

Receive instant crop diagnostics, watch educational video clips, record inputs, and list harvest produce directly.

Produce Traders

Gain direct visibility into local harvest schedules, volume availability, and farmgate pricing in real time.

Extension Officers

Publish verified agronomic guides, track regional pest reports, and conduct community education digitally.
